# Line Art Colors

### 👉 [Open Line Art Colors](https://clankert.github.io/line-art-colors/)

Pick a fill color and get the matching line art, shadow and highlight colors for cartoon-style vector drawings.

## What it does

- Gives four colors for every fill: **line art**, **shadow**, **your color** and **highlight**
- Click any color to copy its hex code, or copy all four at once
- Choose a color with the color picker, a hex code, or one of the quick picks
- Pick a color straight from your screen (Chrome and Edge)
- Adjust line darkness, saturation, hue shift, shadow depth and highlight strength, then reset to the defaults with one click
- Save the colors for each part of your drawing (stored in your browser)

## How the colors are made

| Color | How it's made |
| --- | --- |
| Line art | About 20% darker and 1.5× more saturated, with the hue nudged slightly toward purple or red |
| Shadow | A smaller step in the same direction as the line art |
| Highlight | Lighter and a little less saturated |

Example: green **#4CAF50** gives line art **#137C22**, shadow **#329D3C** and highlight **#73C974**.

## Use it offline

The whole tool is a single file, index.html. Download it and open it in any browser. Nothing to install.
